FROM CLASS PROJECT TO CLICKABLE CONCEPT

A simpler way to choose lunch.

MacroMap began as Mostaque Tahamid’s Entrepreneurship 3310 project: help Houston students and young professionals find restaurant meals that fit their own nutrition goals, budget, and day.

01Set your goalsFilter food and drinks by nutrition, budget, distance, and food preferences.

02See the tradeoffsCompare up to three items and inspect their sample nutrition.

03Make it a mealPair a meal and a drink, review combined totals, then find real delivery or pickup options.

What is real in this prototype?

The area selector, search, filters, goal fit labels, protein value sorting, suggested filter changes, saved favorites and meal builder work. Restaurants, nutrition, prices, caffeine, distances, and source records are fictional. Your selected area, favorites and meal plan are saved on this browser. Area presets update sample distances and nearby results; they do not use your device location.

DoorDash and Uber Eats links open their Houston browsing pages. Pickup search uses Google Maps. Items are not transferred, no order is placed here, and no restaurant or delivery partnership is implied.

This prototype was built with AI assistance. A future service would need current restaurant data and documented nutrition sources.
